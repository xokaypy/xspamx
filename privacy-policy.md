# Privacy Policy for IG Bulk DM Sender

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
IG Bulk DM Sender does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, Instagram credentials, or message content with external servers or third parties.

## 2. Local Browser Storage
All user inputs—including custom message text, target username lists, delay settings, and queue progress—are stored locally on your device using the browser's `chrome.storage` API. This data never leaves your browser and can be cleared at any time by removing the extension or clearing browser site data.

## 3. Permissions
- **activeTab, scripting & Host Permissions (instagram.com):** Used solely to display the extension interface and perform user-initiated messaging actions on Instagram.
- **storage:** Used solely to save your preferences and queue state locally.

## 4. Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the extension's store page.
